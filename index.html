About Me

Hello! My name is Alyicia Wright!

I am a digital media artist whose practice combines illustration, photography, video, design, and creative coding. My work explores the relationship between technology, creativity, and audience engagement through both static and interactive experiences. Using tools such as web design, p5.js, and digital imaging, I create projects that encourage exploration, participation, and curiosity.

Across my work, I investigate how visual storytelling and interactivity can shape the way people experience art. By blending traditional artistic principles with emerging technologies, I create pieces that challenge viewers to engage with art in dynamic and meaningful ways. Whether through digital illustrations, experimental websites, or interactive coding projects, my goal is to create experiences that are both visually engaging and thought-provoking
